WORKOUT SUMMARY · WEEK 7

Powerlifting foundations.

A focused session for confident competition lifts. Review the coaching cues and watch an indicative YouTube video for every exercise before you start.

75 min18 working sets5 exercises2–3 min rest
01 · MAIN LIFT

Competition back squat

Set the bar securely, brace before you descend, keep the knees tracking over the toes and drive evenly through the whole foot.

4 sets5 reps2–3 min rest
02 · MAIN LIFT

Paused bench press

Plant the feet, pin the shoulders to the bench and hold the bar motionless on the chest before pressing with intent.

4 sets5 reps2–3 min rest
03 · MAIN LIFT

Competition deadlift

Bring the bar over mid-foot, take the slack out, brace hard and push the floor away before finishing tall.

4 sets3 reps2–3 min rest
04 · ACCESSORY

Chest-supported row

Keep the chest supported, let the shoulder blades reach forward and drive the elbows back without lifting the torso.

3 sets10 reps90 sec rest
05 · CORE

Weighted plank

Stack the elbows beneath the shoulders, squeeze the glutes and brace hard while keeping a straight line from head to heel.

3 sets30 sec60 sec rest
